### Retail Compliance & Security Assessment — Liquor Retail Environment

**Status:** Coming Soon 🚧

## Overview

Project SpiritGuard is a compliance and security assessment built around a real-world retail liquor environment. Unlike other portfolio projects built from hypothetical job-description scenarios, this assessment is grounded in firsthand operational exposure to a small-to-midsize retail point-of-sale (POS) and alcohol-licensing environment.

The project demonstrates how GRC and RMF-style thinking translates outside of DoD/federal contexts — applying structured risk assessment, control mapping, and policy development to a commercial retail setting governed by payment card security requirements and state alcohol regulation.

## Why This Project

Retail alcohol sales sit at the intersection of three distinct compliance domains that rarely get examined together:

- **Payment card security** (PCI DSS v4.0)
- **State/local alcohol licensing law** (Alcohol Beverage Control requirements)
- **Operational internal controls** (cash handling, inventory, loss prevention)

This project treats that intersection as a single risk landscape rather than three disconnected checklists.

## Frameworks & Standards Applied

| Domain | Framework/Standard |
|---|---|
| Payment environment | PCI DSS v4.0 |
| Alcohol licensing & sales compliance | State ABC (Alcohol Beverage Control) requirements |
| Overarching risk structure | NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) — lightweight adaptation |
| Internal controls | Cash handling & inventory/loss-prevention control design |

## Scope

- POS/payment card environment (cardholder data flow, segmentation, access control)
- Age-verification process (point-of-sale procedure, employee training, audit logging)
- State alcohol licensing conditions (sales-hours compliance, licensing renewal requirements)
- Physical and operational controls (cash handling, inventory shrinkage, loss prevention)

*Out of scope: e-commerce/online sales channels, third-party delivery platform integrations.*

## Planned Deliverables

1. **Risk Assessment Report** — POS environment and physical store risk analysis
2. **PCI DSS v4.0 Gap Assessment Matrix**
3. **Age-Verification Policy & Procedure Document** — including training and audit-logging requirements
4. **Control Traceability Matrix** — mapping ABC, PCI DSS, and NIST CSF controls to a single control set
5. **Executive Summary Dashboard** — POS transaction anomaly monitoring concept (Splunk-style visualization)

## Key Talking Points for Interviews

- Only project in the portfolio built from direct operational observation rather than a simulated JD-driven scenario
- Demonstrates translation of DoD RMF discipline to a commercial, non-federal control environment
- Shows ability to reconcile multiple overlapping compliance regimes (payment security + state regulatory law) into one coherent control framework
