## Project Overview

SpiritGuard is a fictional compliance assessment for a regional liquor store chain operating across multiple locations. The platform processes credit card payments at point-of-sale terminals, manages age verification compliance, and handles state Alcohol Beverage Control (ABC) regulatory requirements. The project demonstrates multi-framework compliance in a retail environment — where payment card security, state licensing requirements, and cybersecurity governance overlap on shared retail infrastructure.

This project is informed by real-world retail compliance experience and demonstrates GRC skills applicable to the retail, hospitality, and small-to-medium business sectors.

---

## Scenario

> SpiritGuard Spirits & Wine is a regional liquor retailer operating 8 locations across two states. The business processes credit card payments at all locations through a shared point-of-sale system and has recently been flagged by their acquiring bank for a PCI-DSS compliance review. Simultaneously the state ABC board has issued new cybersecurity guidance for licensed alcohol retailers. As the GRC Analyst you must assess compliance across all applicable frameworks and build a remediation roadmap.

## Why This Project

Most GRC portfolios focus exclusively on federal or enterprise environments. SpiritGuard demonstrates that compliance frameworks apply at every level — from a DoD system processing classified data to a regional retailer processing credit cards. The skills are the same. The frameworks are adapted to the environment. A GRC professional who can operate in both worlds is significantly more versatile than one who only knows federal compliance.
